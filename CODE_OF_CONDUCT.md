# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work
rather than the person, and make room for people with less context than you.
Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated in any project space.

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks these expectations, contact
the maintainers privately through the repository's security advisory form or
by email to the address listed on the organisation profile. Reports are handled
confidentially.
